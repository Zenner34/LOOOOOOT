==================================================

MOTHER SHAHRAZ

==================================================



Creature Type:

Demon



Demonslaying Elixir:

Yes





==================================================

OVERVIEW

==================================================



Mother Shahraz is one of the final encounters in Black

Temple and serves as a major execution check.



Rather than relying on Shadow Resistance gear, our strategy

focuses on maximizing raid DPS while using Rocket Boots to

consistently handle Fatal Attraction.



Success depends on disciplined movement, proper positioning,

and quick reactions to mechanics.



This fight is won through execution, not raw healing or DPS.





==================================================

RAID PREPARATION

==================================================



REQUIRED



- Every raider must bring Rocket Boots.

- Rocket Boots are mandatory for this strategy.

- Healthstones should be available for every player.

- Personal defensive cooldowns should be saved for Fatal

  Attraction when possible.



RECOMMENDED



- Demonslaying Elixir.

- Free Action Potion (optional).

- Standard DPS consumables.





==================================================

POSITIONING

==================================================



Tank



- Position Mother Shahraz in the fountain. Raid get under your statues ASAP 

- Face her away from the raid.

- Maintain consistent positioning throughout the encounter.



Melee



- Stack behind the boss.

- Minimize unnecessary movement.

- Return quickly after mechanics.



Ranged



- Spread around the rear of the boss while remaining within

  healer range.

- Avoid excessive spacing that complicates healing.



Healers



- Maintain coverage across the raid.

- Be prepared for large bursts of raid damage following

  Fatal Attraction.





==================================================

KEY MECHANICS

==================================================



FATAL ATTRACTION



Effect:



Three random players are teleported together and linked,

taking heavy Shadow damage based on their proximity.



Execution:



- Immediately activate Rocket Boots.

- Sprint directly away from the other two affected players.

- Continue separating until all three players are safely

  spread apart.

- Once the debuff expires, return to your assigned position.

- Use Healthstones or personal defensives if necessary.



Rocket Boots are mandatory for this strategy and replace the

need for Shadow Resistance gear.





SINFUL BEAM



Effect:



Mother Shahraz periodically casts one of several beams that

infairlict raid-wide magical effects.



Possible Beams



Sinful Beam

- Heavy Shadow damage.



Vile Beam

- Poison damage over time.



Wicked Beam

- Arcane damage and mana burn.



Sinister Beam

- Fire damage over time.



Execution



- Healers react quickly to affected players.

- Continue normal positioning.

- Avoid unnecessary movement.

- Personal defensives may be used during dangerous overlaps.





SABER LASH



Effect:



Massive Physical damage split between players in front of

the boss.



Execution:



- Maintain the assigned tank stack at all times.

- Tanks remain tightly grouped.

- Never allow Saber Lash to strike a single player.

- DPS and healers must never stand in front of the boss.





PRISMATIC AURAS



Effect:



Mother cycles through different magical resistance auras

throughout the encounter.



Execution



- Continue normal execution.

- No changes in positioning are required.

- Maintain maximum DPS uptime regardless of the active aura.





==================================================

ENCOUNTER FLOW

==================================================



1. Tanks establish Mother Shahraz in the center of the room.

2. Raid establishes normal positioning.

3. Handle Saber Lash with the tank group.

4. Continue steady DPS while avoiding unnecessary movement.

5. Fatal Attraction occurs.

6. Affected players immediately Rocket Boot away from each

   other.

7. Heal through beam damage.

8. Return to assigned positions.

9. Repeat until Mother Shahraz is defeated.





==================================================

COMMON WIPE CAUSES

==================================================



- Delayed Rocket Boot usage.

- Players remaining stacked after Fatal Attraction.

- Tank group failing Saber Lash positioning.

- Standing in front of the boss.

- Slow recovery after Fatal Attraction.

- Healers falling behind following beam combinations.

- Players panicking instead of moving directly away.





==================================================

PROGRESSION NOTES

==================================================



- Rocket Boots are required for every raid member.

- Shadow Resistance gear is not required for this strategy.

- The faster Fatal Attraction is resolved, the easier the

  encounter becomes.

- Movement should always be deliberate. Running through

  other players during Fatal Attraction creates unnecessary

  damage.

- Save personal defensives for dangerous overlaps or poor

  Fatal Attraction positioning.

- This is one of the easiest fights in Black Temple once

  Fatal Attraction execution becomes consistent.
