==================================================
TERON GOREFIEND
==================================================

Creature Type:
Humanoid

Demonslaying Elixir:
No


==================================================
OVERVIEW
==================================================

Teron Gorefiend is primarily a personal responsibility
encounter.

The boss himself is mechanically simple, but players chosen
for Shadow of Death must successfully control their Spirit
to prevent the raid from being overwhelmed.

One player failing the Spirit phase can quickly result in a
wipe.

The goal is to maintain clean positioning, maximize uptime,
and execute every Shadow of Death correctly.


==================================================
RAID PREPARATION
==================================================

REQUIRED

- Every player should understand the Spirit phase before the
  pull.
- Players assigned Shadow of Death should know the Spirit
  abilities.
- Tanks establish a clean taunt rotation if required.
- Healthstones should be available for every raider.

RECOMMENDED

- Personal defensive cooldowns.
- Standard DPS consumables.
- Demonslaying Elixir is NOT required.


==================================================
POSITIONING
==================================================

Tank

- Position Teron near the center of the room.
- Face him away from the raid.
- Maintain consistent positioning throughout the encounter.

Melee

- Stack behind the boss.
- Maintain maximum uptime.
- Return immediately after mechanics.

Ranged

- Spread loosely around the boss.
- Stay within healer range.
- Avoid unnecessary movement.

Healers

- Maintain full raid coverage.
- Prepare for Shadow of Death targets.
- Be ready for Incinerate damage.


==================================================
KEY MECHANICS
==================================================

SHADOW OF DEATH

Effect:

Every 30 seconds Teron selects four random non-tank players.

After 55 seconds they die, becoming a Vengeful Spirit.

Execution:

- If selected, continue normal DPS until death.
- Prepare to immediately control your Spirit.
- Do not panic.
- Continue executing Spirit mechanics until your timer
  expires.

This mechanic determines whether the raid kills the boss.


==================================================
VENGEFUL SPIRIT
==================================================

OVERVIEW

Upon death you become a Vengeful Spirit.

Your only responsibility is eliminating the Shadowy
Constructs before they reach the raid.


SPIRIT ABILITIES
==================================================

SPIRIT STRIKE

- Primary attack.
- High Physical damage.
- Use on every Construct.

SPIRIT CHAINS

- Roots Constructs in place.
- Use to control multiple Constructs.

SPIRIT LANCE

- Heavy ranged damage.
- Use whenever available.

SPIRIT VOLLEY

- AoE damage.
- Useful when several Constructs are grouped together.


SPIRIT PRIORITY
==================================================

1. Root incoming Constructs.
2. Kill nearest Construct.
3. Continue eliminating every Construct.
4. Never allow Constructs to reach the raid.


==================================================
SHADOWY CONSTRUCTS
==================================================

Effect:

Constructs spawn only for Vengeful Spirits.

If they reach the raid they will rapidly kill players.

Execution:

- Root first.
- Kill immediately.
- Continue moving while attacking.
- Never allow Constructs to stack together.


==================================================
INCINERATE
==================================================

Effect:

Random Fire damage on a raid member.

Execution:

- Healers react quickly.
- Continue normal positioning.
- No special movement required.


==================================================
CRUSHING SHADOWS
==================================================

Effect:

Shadow damage debuff applied to random players.

Execution:

- Healers monitor affected players.
- Continue normal execution.


==================================================
POSITIONING DURING SHADOW OF DEATH
==================================================

Raid

- Continue normal positioning.
- Do not chase Spirit players.
- Continue DPS.

Spirit Players

- Focus exclusively on killing Constructs.
- Ignore the raid.
- Keep Constructs controlled until Spirit expires.


==================================================
ENCOUNTER FLOW
==================================================

1. Tank establishes Teron.
2. Raid spreads into assigned positions.
3. Maintain maximum DPS uptime.
4. Shadow of Death targets continue normal play until
   death.
5. Dead players immediately control their Spirit.
6. Kill every Shadowy Construct.
7. Continue boss damage.
8. Repeat until Teron dies.


==================================================
COMMON WIPE CAUSES
==================================================

- Players unfamiliar with Spirit controls.
- Constructs reaching the raid.
- Spirit players panicking.
- Standing in front of the boss.
- Poor healer reaction to Incinerate.
- Raid losing focus after multiple Spirit phases.


==================================================
PROGRESSION NOTES
==================================================

- This encounter is not a DPS race.
- Individual execution is far more important than overall
  raid damage.
- Every raider should practice the Spirit abilities before
  progression.
- If every Shadow of Death player successfully controls
  their Constructs, Teron becomes one of the easiest bosses
  in Black Temple.
- Continue maximizing boss uptime while Spirit players
  handle their assignment independently.
