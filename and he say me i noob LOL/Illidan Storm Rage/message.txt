==================================================
ILLIDAN STORMRAGE
==================================================

Creature Type:
Demon

Demonslaying Elixir:
Yes


==================================================
OVERVIEW
==================================================

Illidan is the final encounter of Black Temple and rewards
discipline more than raw DPS.

The encounter consists of multiple phases that build on one
another, but the majority of progression wipes occur during
Flames of Azzinoth.

Our goal is to execute every phase cleanly, minimize
movement, and push Illidan hard enough to eliminate or
significantly shorten Demon Phase.

Clean mechanics kill this boss.


==================================================
PREPARATION
==================================================

Required

- Demonslaying Elixir
- Healthstone
- Standard DPS consumables

Recommended

- Free Action Potion (optional)
- Rocket Boots (useful during progression)
- Personal defensive cooldowns assigned


==================================================
PHASE ONE
==================================================

Overview

A straightforward tank-and-spank encounter.

The objective is to maximize uptime while keeping the arena
clean before the most dangerous phase begins.


KEY MECHANICS
==================================================

SHEAR

Effect

Massively reduces the tank's maximum health.

Execution

- Tanks must avoid being hit by Shear.
- Defensive cooldowns should be planned.
- A successful Shear will usually result in a tank death.


FLAME CRASH

Effect

Creates a fire patch beneath a player's location.

Execution

- Move immediately.
- Return to position once safe.
- Never drag fire through the raid.


PARASITIC SHADOWFIEND

Effect

Infests a random player.

Execution

- The affected player immediately moves away from the raid.
- Parasites are picked up and killed immediately.
- Never allow Parasites to spawn inside the raid.


POSITIONING
==================================================

Tank

- Keep Illidan near the center.
- Face him away from the raid.

Melee

- Maximum uptime behind the boss.

Ranged

- Spread naturally while remaining in healer range.

Raid

- Minimize unnecessary movement.


==================================================
PHASE TWO
FLAMES OF AZZINOTH
==================================================

Overview

This is the progression phase.

The encounter is won or lost here.

The objective is to control Blaze, avoid Eye Beam, and kill
the Flames as quickly as possible.


FLAME TANKS
==================================================

Each Flame receives its own dedicated tank.

Execution

- Tanks continuously kite their Flame around the outside of
  the platform.
- Blaze is dropped wherever a Flame stands.
- Never allow Blaze to stack in one location.
- Kite smoothly and preserve as much room as possible.
- Never over-kite.


EYE BEAM

Execution

- Move immediately.
- Rotate around the outside edge.
- Never run through the middle.
- Resume DPS immediately after reaching safety.


BLAZE

Execution

- Blaze permanently removes usable space.
- Tanks are responsible for managing platform space.
- Smooth movement is always better than panic movement.


DPS STRATEGY
==================================================

Our Strategy

- Entire raid hard commits to Flame #1.
- Kill Flame #1.
- Immediately hard swap to Flame #2.
- Never split DPS.

This shortens the most dangerous phase of the encounter and
reduces overall Blaze coverage.


COMMON MISTAKES
==================================================

- Splitting DPS.
- Standing in Blaze.
- Crossing Eye Beam.
- Tanks allowing Flames to sit still.
- Panic movement.


==================================================
PHASE THREE
==================================================

Overview

Illidan returns to normal mechanics.

This phase should feel identical to Phase One with continued
Parasite management.

Execution

- Continue handling Flame Crash.
- Parasite targets move away immediately.
- Kill Parasites quickly.
- Maximize boss uptime.


==================================================
DEMON PHASE
==================================================

Goal

Skip this phase whenever possible through strong raid DPS.

If Demon Phase occurs:

Warlock Tank

- Equip Shadow Resistance gear.
- Maintain threat using Searing Pain.
- Stay within healer range.
- Maintain consistent positioning.

Raid

- Kill Shadow Demons immediately.
- Continue normal positioning.
- Return to Illidan as soon as Demons are dead.

A clean raid should spend very little time in this phase.


==================================================
FINAL PHASE
==================================================

Overview

Illidan combines previous mechanics into one final burn.

Execution

- Continue handling Flame Crash.
- Continue handling Parasites.
- Kill Shadow Demons immediately if they appear.
- Maintain positioning.
- Use remaining offensive cooldowns.
- Do not greed mechanics for extra DPS.

==================================================
COMMON WIPE CAUSES
==================================================

- Tank dying to Shear.
- Parasites spawning inside the raid.
- Splitting DPS on Flames.
- Poor Flame kiting.
- Eye Beam deaths.
- Blaze covering too much of the platform.
- Panic during transitions.
- Greeding DPS instead of respecting mechanics.


==================================================
PROGRESSION NOTES
==================================================

- Flames of Azzinoth determine the outcome of the encounter.
- Hard commit to one Flame before swapping.
- Blaze management is more important than maximizing DPS.
- Every player should prioritize mechanics over personal
  damage.
- A clean Flames phase makes the remainder of the fight
  significantly easier.
- With strong execution and raid DPS, Demon Phase becomes
  extremely short or can be skipped entirely.
