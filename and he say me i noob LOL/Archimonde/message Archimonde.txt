==================================================
ARCHIMONDE
==================================================

Creature Type:
Demon

Demonslaying Elixir:
Yes


OVERVIEW
==================================================

Archimonde is the final encounter of Mount Hyjal and one of
the most punishing fights in The Burning Crusade.

Unlike previous encounters, Archimonde is not a DPS check.
The boss dies when every player consistently executes the
mechanics correctly.

Almost every mechanic can wipe the raid if handled
incorrectly. Surviving is more important than maximizing
damage.

Stay calm, trust the strategy, and execute one mechanic at
a time.


KEY MECHANICS
==================================================

AIR BURST

Effect:

Launches a random player high into the air, dealing heavy
fall damage upon landing.

Execution:

- Use Tears of the Goddess before landing.
- Activate Tears near the peak of the knock-up to negate
  fall damage.
- Return to your assigned position immediately after
  landing.
- Every player should have Tears of the Goddess on their
  action bars before the pull.


DOOMFIRE

Effect:

Summons a moving trail of fire that follows random players.

Execution:

- Move early.
- Kite the Doomfire away from the raid.
- Never run Doomfire through melee or healers.
- Small movements are preferred over large panic movements.
- Once safe, immediately return to your original position.
- Maintain awareness of nearby Doomfire while repositioning.


FEAR

Effect:

Mass fear cast throughout the encounter.

Execution:

- Tremor Totems should be rotated to provide continuous
  coverage for melee and tanks.
- Fear Ward should be maintained on the active tank whenever
  available.
- Do not spread farther than necessary from Tremor Totems.
- Recover positioning immediately after every Fear.


GRIP OF THE LEGION

Effect:

Applies a Shadow damage-over-time effect to a random
player.

Execution:

- Healers quickly stabilize affected players.
- Continue normal positioning.
- Do not force unnecessary movement.


FINGER OF DEATH

Effect:

Massive damage if no player is within melee range.

Execution:

- Tanks must maintain contact with Archimonde at all times.
- Never allow Archimonde to lose his primary target.
- Prevent unnecessary tank movement during mechanics.


SOUL CHARGE

Effect:

Whenever a player dies, Archimonde gains a powerful buff
based on that player's class.

Execution:

- Deaths are not recoverable mistakes.
- Every player should prioritize survival over DPS.
- Avoid unnecessary risks throughout the encounter.


POSITIONING
==================================================

- Tank Archimonde near the center of the encounter area.
- Face the boss away from the raid.
- Melee remain behind the boss.
- Ranged and healers spread evenly around the encounter
  while remaining within healing range.
- Avoid creating large gaps that make Doomfire movement
  unpredictable.
- Return to your assigned location after every mechanic.
- Clean positioning makes every subsequent mechanic easier.


ENCOUNTER FLOW
==================================================

Pull

↓

Establish boss positioning.

↓

Maintain consistent DPS while preserving clean raid
spacing.

↓

Handle Doomfire without disrupting raid positioning.

↓

Players affected by Air Burst safely land using Tears of
the Goddess.

↓

Recover after Fear.

↓

Repeat until Archimonde reaches low health.

↓

Continue executing every mechanic exactly the same during
the final burn.

↓

Victory.


FINAL BURN
==================================================

Archimonde does not become more mechanically difficult at
low health.

The temptation is to ignore mechanics and finish the boss.

Do not.

Continue handling:

- Doomfire.
- Air Burst.
- Fear.
- Grip of the Legion.
- Positioning.

A clean final minute is far more valuable than attempting
to rush the kill.

If everyone survives, Archimonde dies.


COMMON WIPE CAUSES
==================================================

- Forgetting to use Tears of the Goddess after Air Burst.
- Doomfire being dragged through the raid.
- Players panicking during Doomfire movement.
- Poor Tremor Totem or Fear Ward coverage.
- Tank losing contact with Archimonde.
- Deaths causing dangerous Soul Charge buffs.
- Players sacrificing mechanics for additional DPS.
- Raid collapsing into poor positions after movement.
- Chain deaths during the final burn.


FINAL NOTES
==================================================

Archimonde rewards discipline more than damage.

Every mechanic has a solution.

Trust the strategy.

Respect every mechanic.

Stay alive.

If everyone executes consistently, Archimonde will fall.
