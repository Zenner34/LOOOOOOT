
==================================================
ANETHERON
==================================================

Creature Type:
Demon

Demonslaying Elixir:
Yes


OVERVIEW
==================================================

Anetheron is primarily an add management encounter. While
the boss has dangerous abilities, Infernal control is what
determines whether the raid succeeds.

The raid should remain calm during Infernal spawns and
avoid sacrificing positioning while switching targets.


KEY MECHANICS
==================================================

INFERNO

Effect:

Summons an Infernal that deals heavy Fire damage and
creates constant raid pressure.

Execution:

- Immediately identify the Infernal landing location.
- Tanks establish control quickly.
- Ranged DPS immediately eliminate the Infernal.
- Resume boss damage once the Infernal dies.


CARRION SWARM

Effect:

Heavy frontal Shadow damage.

Execution:

- Boss must remain faced away from the raid.
- Never stand in front of Anetheron.


SLEEP

Effect:

Places multiple players into a magical sleep.

Execution:

- Healers should anticipate temporary healing gaps.
- Tanks must remain prepared for incoming damage while
  portions of the raid are incapacitated.


VAMPIRIC AURA

Effect:

Anetheron heals through melee attacks.

Execution:

- Maintain steady DPS.
- Minimize unnecessary encounter length.


POSITIONING
==================================================

- Tank the boss facing away from the raid.
- Melee remain behind the boss.
- Ranged spread enough to avoid Infernal overlap.
- Maintain awareness of Infernal landing locations.


ENCOUNTER FLOW
==================================================

1. Establish positioning.
2. Maintain boss damage.
3. Swap immediately to Infernals.
4. Return to boss DPS.
5. Repeat until defeated.


COMMON WIPE CAUSES
==================================================

- Ignoring Infernal spawns.
- Standing inside Infernal fire.
- Carrion Swarm hitting the raid.
- Tanks losing control after Sleep.
