==================================================
KAZ'ROGAL
==================================================

Creature Type:
Demon

Demonslaying Elixir:
Yes


OVERVIEW
==================================================

Kaz'rogal is a resource management encounter that heavily
punishes mana users. While the mechanics are relatively
simple, poor mana management or overextending cooldowns can
quickly wipe the raid.

The encounter becomes easier the faster it ends, making
efficient DPS and proper cooldown usage important without
sacrificing execution.


KEY MECHANICS
==================================================

MARK OF KAZ'ROGAL

Effect:

Applies a debuff that periodically drains mana. Players who
reach 0 mana explode, dealing heavy damage to themselves and
nearby allies.

Execution:

- Mana users should closely monitor their mana pool.
- Avoid reaching 0 mana.
- Use Mana Potions, Dark Runes, Demonic Runes, Mana Tide,
  Innervate, and other mana restoration abilities
  proactively.
- Healers should avoid excessive overhealing to preserve
  mana.
- If you are close to empty mana, communicate before it
  becomes a problem.


CLEAVE

Effect:

Heavy frontal physical attack.

Execution:

- Keep Kaz'rogal faced away from the raid.
- Never stand in front of the boss unless assigned.


WAR STOMP

Effect:

Short-range stun affecting nearby players.

Execution:

- Tanks should maintain stable positioning.
- Healers prepare for brief healing disruption.


POSITIONING
==================================================

- Tank Kaz'rogal near the entrance.
- Face the boss away from the raid.
- Melee remain behind the boss.
- Ranged and healers spread while remaining within healing
  range.


ENCOUNTER FLOW
==================================================

1. Establish boss positioning.
2. Maintain steady DPS.
3. Manage mana carefully throughout the fight.
4. Use mana cooldowns before reaching critical levels.
5. Continue until the boss is defeated.


COMMON WIPE CAUSES
==================================================

- Players exploding from Mark of Kaz'rogal.
- Running completely out of mana.
- Excessive overhealing early in the encounter.
- Standing in front of the boss.
- Panic usage of mana cooldowns after reaching critical
  mana levels.
