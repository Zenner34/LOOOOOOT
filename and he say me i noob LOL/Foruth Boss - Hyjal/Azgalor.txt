==================================================
AZGALOR
==================================================

Creature Type:
Demon

Demonslaying Elixir:
Yes


OVERVIEW
==================================================

Azgalor is the final encounter before Archimonde and serves
as a positioning and personal responsibility check.

Every player must execute mechanics correctly. Doom and Rain
of Fire punish individual mistakes, while Howl of Azgalor
creates constant pressure on healers.

The encounter is controlled through discipline and proper
movement.


KEY MECHANICS
==================================================

DOOM

Effect:

Targets a random player with a debuff that cannot be
removed. When the debuff expires, the player dies and a Doom
Guard is summoned.

Execution:

- Affected players should immediately move away from the
  raid.
- Do not die inside the raid.
- Tanks should immediately pick up the Doom Guard.
- Ranged DPS quickly eliminate the Doom Guard before
  returning to the boss.


RAIN OF FIRE

Effect:

Creates a large area of Fire damage.

Execution:

- Move immediately.
- Never finish a cast while standing in Rain of Fire.
- Return to position once safe.


HOWL OF AZGALOR

Effect:

Raid-wide silence affecting nearby players.

Execution:

- Healers should prepare before each silence.
- Avoid unnecessary damage immediately before the silence
  occurs.
- Personal defensives should be used if healing is delayed.


CLEAVE

Effect:

Heavy frontal physical damage.

Execution:

- Keep Azgalor facing away from the raid.
- Never stand in front of the boss unless assigned.


POSITIONING
==================================================

- Tank Azgalor facing away from the raid.
- Melee remain behind the boss.
- Ranged spread around the encounter area.
- Players affected by Doom immediately leave the raid before
  dying.
- Doom Guards should be picked up away from the raid before
  being burned down.


ENCOUNTER FLOW
==================================================

1. Establish positioning.
2. Maintain steady boss damage.
3. Handle Rain of Fire immediately.
4. Doom targets move away from the raid.
5. Tanks secure Doom Guards.
6. Kill Doom Guards.
7. Resume boss damage.
8. Repeat until Azgalor is defeated.


COMMON WIPE CAUSES
==================================================

- Doom targets dying inside the raid.
- Tanks failing to pick up Doom Guards.
- Standing in Rain of Fire.
- Healers being caught by Howl after unnecessary raid
  damage.
- Players standing in front of the boss.
