==================================================
HIGH WARLORD NAJ'ENTUS
==================================================

Creature Type:
Demon

Demonslaying Elixir:
Yes

==================================================
OVERVIEW
==================================================

Naj'entus is primarily a healer and execution check.

The fight revolves around managing Tidal Shield quickly while
maintaining a spread to reduce raid damage.

The faster the shield is removed, the easier the encounter
becomes.

==================================================
PREPARATION
==================================================

Required

- Demonslaying Elixir
- Healthstone
- Standard DPS consumables

Recommended

- Personal defensives

==================================================
POSITIONING
==================================================

Tank

- Tank Naj'entus near the center.
- Face him away from the raid.
- Minimize unnecessary movement.

Raid

- Spread evenly around the boss.
- Remain within healing range.
- Avoid stacking whenever possible.

Healers

- Expect constant raid-wide damage.
- Be prepared for shield breaks.

==================================================
KEY MECHANICS
==================================================

IMPALING SPINE

Effect

Random player is impaled and takes heavy Physical damage.

Execution

- Heal target immediately.
- Loot the Spine immediately.
- Assigned player keeps the Spine until Tidal Shield.

Never throw the Spine early.

--------------------------------------------------

TIDAL SHIELD

Effect

Naj'entus becomes immune and rapidly heals.

Execution

- Wait until the raid is healthy.
- Assigned player throws the Spine immediately.
- Shield breaks.
- Heroism/Bloodlust if assigned.
- Resume full DPS instantly.

The shield should be removed as quickly as safely possible.

--------------------------------------------------

NEEDLE SPINE

Effect

Physical damage to several nearby players.

Execution

- Maintain spread.
- Do not stack.
- Healers prepare for raid damage.

--------------------------------------------------

ENRAGE

Hard enrage after eight minutes.

Maintain strong DPS uptime.

==================================================
ENCOUNTER FLOW
==================================================

1. Spread around the boss.
2. Heal Impaling Spine targets.
3. Loot every Spine.
4. Continue DPS.
5. Wait for Tidal Shield.
6. Break Shield immediately.
7. Resume DPS.
8. Repeat until kill.

==================================================
COMMON WIPE CAUSES
==================================================

- Throwing Spine early.
- No Spine available for Tidal Shield.
- Slow Shield break.
- Poor raid spread.
- Deaths before Shield break.
- Low DPS causing enrage.

==================================================
PROGRESSION NOTES
==================================================

- Never panic throw a Spine.
- Keep one assigned player responsible for every Shield.
- Shield break timing is more important than maximizing DPS.
- Stay spread throughout the encounter.
- Resume DPS immediately after every Shield.

==================================================
END OF HIGH WARLORD NAJ'ENTUS
==================================================
