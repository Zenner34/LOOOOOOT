==================================================
GURTOGG BLOODBOIL
==================================================

Creature Type:
Humanoid

Demonslaying Elixir:
No


==================================================
OVERVIEW
==================================================

Gurtogg Bloodboil is one of the first major progression
checks inside Black Temple.

The encounter is won through clean Bloodboil rotations,
proper tank swaps, and disciplined healing during Fel Rage.

This is primarily a healing and execution fight rather than
a DPS check. Consistent positioning and communication will
result in a much smoother kill. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}


==================================================
PREPARATION
==================================================

- Three Bloodboil soak groups (5 players each) are required.
- Four groups are ideal if your raid composition allows.
- Three tanks are strongly recommended for progression.
- Assign Bloodboil groups before the pull.
- Assign tank swap order.
- Assign healer coverage for each tank and the Fel Rage
  target.
- Heroism/Bloodlust is recommended during the first Fel Rage
  for maximum raid DPS. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}


==================================================
PHASE ONE
==================================================

OVERVIEW

Phase One lasts approximately 60 seconds and revolves around
Bloodboil rotations, tank management, and positioning.

This phase repeats after every Fel Rage.


==================================================
KEY MECHANICS
==================================================

BLOODBOIL

Effect:

Every 10-12 seconds, Gurtogg applies Bloodboil to the five
players furthest away from him.

The debuff stacks and quickly becomes lethal if rotations
are missed.

Execution:

- Assigned Bloodboil group moves to the soak position.
- After receiving Bloodboil, immediately return to the raid.
- The next group rotates into position.
- Repeat until Fel Rage begins.
- Never accidentally become one of the five furthest
  players unless assigned. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}


ACIDIC WOUND

Effect:

Heavy armor reduction and bleed applied to the active tank.

Execution:

- Tanks rotate around 8-10 stacks depending on healer
  comfort.
- Allow previous tank's stacks to expire before taunting
  again.
- Protection Paladins may Divine Shield to remove stacks if
  assigned. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}


BEWILDERING STRIKE

Effect:

Reduces the current tank's threat significantly.

Execution:

- Off tanks remain high on threat throughout Phase One.
- Be prepared for immediate threat changes.
- Paladins may use Blessing of Protection on the affected
  tank if needed, then immediately replace the blessing so
  Gurtogg returns to the tank instead of chasing the raid. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}


EJECT

Effect:

Knocks back the active tank and reduces their threat.

Execution:

- Tanks maintain strong threat throughout the phase.
- Off tanks should always remain second and third on threat.
- Be prepared for immediate boss movement after Eject. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}


ARCING SMASH

Effect:

Heavy frontal attack that reduces healing received.

Execution:

- Keep Gurtogg faced away from the raid.
- Only the active tank should ever be struck.


FEL ACID BREATH

Effect:

Heavy frontal Nature damage and damage-over-time effect.

Execution:

- Only the active tank should be hit.
- If targeted as melee, quickly move to the opposite side of
  the boss and return once safe.
- Rogues can Cloak of Shadows to remove the debuff. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}


==================================================
POSITIONING
==================================================

Tank

- Position Gurtogg near the center of the room.
- Face him away from the raid.

Melee

- Stack behind one side of the boss.
- Swap sides only if necessary to avoid Fel Acid Breath.

Ranged

- Maintain assigned Bloodboil positions.
- Rotate only when your soak group is active.

Healers

- Keep assigned tanks covered.
- Prepare for heavy Bloodboil damage on soaking groups.


==================================================
PHASE TWO
FEL RAGE
==================================================

OVERVIEW

After the fifth Bloodboil, Gurtogg enters Fel Rage for 30
seconds.

Threat is ignored during this phase as Gurtogg fixates on a
random player.

This is the highest healing requirement of the encounter.


==================================================
KEY MECHANICS
==================================================

FEL GEYSER

Effect:

Fel Rage begins with Fel Geyser, dealing Nature damage and
knocking back nearby players.

Execution:

- Spread before the fifth Bloodboil.
- Minimize splash damage.
- Return to assigned positions once the knockback resolves. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}


FEL RAGE

Execution:

- The targeted player immediately moves to the Main Tank's
  previous position.
- All healers except one assigned tank healer should swap to
  healing the Fel Rage target.
- Use personal defensives immediately.
- Healthstones, potions, and class defensives should be used
  proactively.
- Hunters, Rogues, and Warlocks should use their defensive
  abilities to help survive if targeted. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}


INSIGNIFICANCE

Effect:

All other players lose threat during Fel Rage.

Execution:

- Tanks contribute DPS.
- DPS players use offensive cooldowns.
- Heroism/Bloodlust is typically used during the first Fel
  Rage.
- Threat is irrelevant until Phase One resumes. :contentReference[oaicite:9]{index=9}


==================================================
POSITIONING
==================================================

Fel Rage Target

- Move to the Main Tank position.
- Remain predictable for healers.

Raid

- Spread around the Fel Rage player.
- Avoid unnecessary Fel Geyser splash damage.

Healers

- Commit almost all healing to the Fel Rage target.
- Continue healing aggressively until the phase ends.


==================================================
ENCOUNTER FLOW
==================================================

1. Establish Phase One positioning.
2. Begin Bloodboil rotations.
3. Rotate tanks as Acidic Wound stacks increase.
4. Maintain strong threat through Bewildering Strike and
   Eject.
5. Spread before the fifth Bloodboil.
6. Fel Rage begins.
7. Fel Rage target moves to the Main Tank position.
8. Raid uses offensive cooldowns.
9. Keep the Fel Rage target alive.
10. Resume Phase One positioning.
11. Repeat until Gurtogg dies.


==================================================
COMMON WIPE CAUSES
==================================================

- Incorrect Bloodboil rotations.
- Players accidentally soaking Bloodboil.
- Tanks too low on threat after Bewildering Strike or Eject.
- Poor Acidic Wound tank swaps.
- Fel Rage target dying.
- Raid stacked for Fel Geyser.
- Players standing in front of Gurtogg.
- Healers failing to swap quickly during Fel Rage.


==================================================
PROGRESSION NOTES
==================================================

- Three tanks make the encounter substantially more stable.
- Communication between Bloodboil groups is essential.
- Tanks should intentionally manage threat so off tanks
  remain close throughout Phase One.
- Heroism/Bloodlust and personal DPS cooldowns are most
  effective during Fel Rage since threat is no longer a
  concern.
- This encounter rewards consistency over greed. Clean
  Bloodboil rotations and disciplined healing are more
  valuable than squeezing out extra DPS. :contentReference[oaicite:10]{index=10}
