==================================================
RAGE WINTERCHILL
==================================================

Creature Type:
Undead

Demonslaying Elixir:
No


OVERVIEW
==================================================

Rage Winterchill is the opening encounter of Mount Hyjal
and serves as a positioning and awareness check. While the
mechanics are simple, poor movement or delayed reactions
quickly overwhelm healers.

The encounter rewards disciplined positioning and immediate
responses to targeted mechanics rather than raw DPS.


KEY MECHANICS
==================================================

ICEBOLT

Effect:

Freezes a random player, dealing heavy Frost damage and
preventing all actions.

Execution:

- Healers should immediately stabilize the affected player.
- Be prepared for temporary healing disruption if a healer
  is targeted.


DEATH AND DECAY

Effect:

Creates a large Shadow damage zone beneath a random player.

Execution:

- Move immediately.
- Never attempt to finish a cast while standing in Death
  and Decay.
- Re-establish positioning once the area is clear.


FROST NOVA

Effect:

Roots nearby players.

Execution:

- Remain aware of Death and Decay placement while rooted.
- Recover positioning immediately once the root expires.


POSITIONING
==================================================

- Tank Winterchill near the center of the fighting area.
- Face the boss away from the raid.
- Melee remain behind the boss.
- Ranged and healers spread to reduce movement conflicts
  while remaining within healing range.


ENCOUNTER FLOW
==================================================

1. Establish boss positioning.
2. Maintain consistent DPS.
3. Immediately move from Death and Decay.
4. Stabilize Icebolt targets.
5. Continue until the boss is defeated.


COMMON WIPE CAUSES
==================================================

- Standing in Death and Decay.
- Delayed movement.
- Frozen players dying before receiving heals.
- Raid collapsing into the same location.
