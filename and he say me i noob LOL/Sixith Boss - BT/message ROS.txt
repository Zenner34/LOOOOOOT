==================================================
RELIQUARY OF SOULS
==================================================

Creature Type:
Elemental

Demonslaying Elixir:
No


OVERVIEW
==================================================

Reliquary of Souls is one of the most mechanically unique
encounters in Black Temple.

The fight consists of three distinct phases, each with its
own mechanics and raid requirements. Every phase demands a
different approach, and clean transitions are just as
important as the phases themselves.

Phase One tests tank execution.

Phase Two is an interrupt and healing check.

Phase Three is a threat and DPS race.

Most wipes occur during Phase Two due to missed interrupts
or poor management of Deaden and Rune Shield. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}


==================================================
PHASE ONE
ESSENCE OF SUFFERING
==================================================

OVERVIEW

Healing is disabled during this phase by Aura of
Suffering. Tanks survive through avoidance and proper tank
swaps rather than healer throughput.

There is effectively no threat after the opening moments,
allowing DPS to go all out once the tank has established
initial control. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}


KEY MECHANICS
==================================================

AURA OF SUFFERING

Effect:

- Reduces healing received by 100%.
- Removes armor.
- Greatly reduces Defense.

Execution:

- Healers should contribute damage instead of attempting
  ineffective healing.
- Priests should keep Power Word: Shield on the active tank.
- Tanks rely on avoidance and cooldowns rather than heals.


FIXATE

Effect:

The closest player becomes the active target for several
seconds.

Execution:

- Tanks perform controlled swaps by becoming the closest
  player.
- Never run through the boss.
- DPS should never be the closest player.


SOUL DRAIN

Effect:

Magic debuff that quickly kills players if left active.

Execution:

- Dispel immediately.
- Priests can Mass Dispel.
- Paladins should assist with Cleanse when available.


ENRAGE

Effect:

Heavy increased melee damage.

Execution:

- Rogue with Evasion can temporarily soak if assigned.
- Otherwise tanks use defensive cooldowns.


POSITIONING
==================================================

- Main tank begins closest to the boss.
- Melee attack from maximum melee range.
- Entire raid stacks behind the boss.
- Nobody should move in front of the boss unless performing
  a planned tank swap.


==================================================
INTERLUDE
==================================================

After each Essence dies, Enslaved Souls begin spawning.

Execution:

- Stack together while killing Souls.
- Soul Release restores health and mana to nearby players.
- Use this time to recover resources.
- Hunters should prepare Misdirection before the next
  Essence becomes active.
- Do not stand too close to the Reliquary until the tank is
  ready to pick up the next phase. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}


==================================================
PHASE TWO
ESSENCE OF DESIRE
==================================================

OVERVIEW

This is the hardest phase of the encounter.

Success depends almost entirely on interrupt discipline,
proper handling of Rune Shield, and controlling Deaden.

Every missed interrupt dramatically increases the chance of
a wipe. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}


KEY MECHANICS
==================================================

AURA OF DESIRE

Effect:

Players take damage equal to a portion of the damage they
deal.

Execution:

- Monitor your health constantly.
- Warlocks should be cautious with Life Tap.
- Shadow Priests should never Shadow Word: Death while
  Deaden is active.


SPIRIT SHOCK

Effect:

Heavy Shadow damage and interrupt lockout.

Execution:

- Assign a dedicated interrupt rotation.
- Never overlap interrupts.
- Missing Spirit Shock casts is the fastest way to wipe.


DEADEN

Effect:

Greatly reduces incoming damage to the boss.

Execution:

- Warriors should Spell Reflect when possible.
- If not reflected, remove it immediately.
- Never continue DPS into Deaden.


RUNE SHIELD

Effect:

Absorbs damage while healing the boss.

Execution:

- Mages should Spellsteal immediately.
- If Spellsteal is unavailable, Priests and Shamans should
  remove it with Dispel Magic or Purge as assigned.
- Do not continue DPS until Rune Shield has been removed.


CAST SPEED REDUCTION

Execution:

- Maintain Curse of Tongues or Mind-numbing Poison
  throughout the phase.
- Slower casts provide a much safer interrupt window for
  Spirit Shock. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}


POSITIONING
==================================================

- Tank keeps the boss stationary.
- Raid remains stacked behind the boss.
- Interrupt players remain within range at all times.
- Avoid unnecessary movement.


==================================================
PHASE THREE
ESSENCE OF ANGER
==================================================

OVERVIEW

This is a controlled burn phase.

Raid damage steadily increases as Aura of Anger ramps up,
turning the encounter into a race between healer mana and
boss health.

Bloodlust/Heroism and remaining offensive cooldowns should
be committed here if your strategy calls for it. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}


KEY MECHANICS
==================================================

AURA OF ANGER

Effect:

Increasing raid-wide Shadow damage over time.

Execution:

- Finish the encounter quickly.
- Healers should expect increasing raid damage throughout
  the phase.


SOUL SCREAM

Effect:

Heavy frontal Shadow damage and mana burn.

Execution:

- Tank faces the boss away from the raid.
- Never stand in front of the boss.
- Tanks should spend Rage frequently to reduce incoming
  damage.


SPITE

Effect:

Marks a player before dealing heavy burst damage.

Execution:

- Healers quickly top the player.
- Personal defensives and Healthstones should be used when
  necessary.


THREAT

Execution:

- Watch threat meters carefully.
- Pulling aggro during this phase frequently results in a
  wipe.


POSITIONING
==================================================

- Tank keeps the boss stationary.
- Melee remain behind the boss.
- Ranged spread as needed while maintaining healer range.
- Nobody should ever stand in front of the boss except the
  active tank.


==================================================
ENCOUNTER FLOW
==================================================

1. Defeat Essence of Suffering.
2. Stack and kill Enslaved Souls during the interlude.
3. Recover health and mana.
4. Defeat Essence of Desire while maintaining perfect
   interrupts.
5. Stack and recover during the second interlude.
6. Defeat Essence of Anger before raid damage overwhelms the
   healers.


==================================================
COMMON WIPE CAUSES
==================================================

- Missed Spirit Shock interrupts.
- Slow removal of Rune Shield.
- DPS attacking through Deaden.
- Failure to maintain Curse of Tongues or Mind-numbing
  Poison.
- Tanks mishandling Fixate in Phase One.
- Standing in front of Essence of Anger.
- Players pulling threat in Phase Three.
- Slow recovery during intermissions.
