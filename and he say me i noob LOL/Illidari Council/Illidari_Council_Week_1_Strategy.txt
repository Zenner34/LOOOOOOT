ILLIDARI COUNCIL - WEEK 1 STRATEGY

Creature Type:
Humanoid

Demonslaying Elixir:
No


OVERVIEW

For our first clear we are intentionally using the safer Council strategy.

We are NOT using the full cleave strategy.

POSITIONING

Gathios + Veras
- Stacked together.
- Physical DPS focuses Veras.
- Melee can cleave between the two.

Malande
- Kept separated from Gathios and Veras.
- Dedicated tank.
- Dedicated Rogue assigned full time.
- Do not bring Malande into the Gathios/Veras group.

Zerevor
- Separated from everyone.
- Mage kites him around the room.


TARGET PRIORITY

PHYSICAL DPS

1. Veras
2. Gathios when Veras Vanishes
3. Veras when he returns

Malande is NOT cleaved during Week 1.

Zerevor is handled by the Mage.


GATHIOS + VERAS

Gathios and Veras remain together.

The Veras tank keeps Veras positioned with Gathios.

When Veras Vanishes:
- Melee immediately swaps to Gathios.
- Continue damaging Gathios.
- Handle Deadly Poison targets.

When Veras returns:
- Off Tank immediately establishes threat.
- Bring Veras back into position.
- Melee immediately returns to Veras.


MALANDE - WEEK 1

Malande remains separated from Gathios and Veras for the entire encounter.

ROGUE

One Rogue is assigned to Malande FULL TIME.

The Rogue does not leave Malande to chase DPS or assist another target unless specifically necessary.

The Rogue should have:
- Mind-Numbing Poison
- PvP gloves

The Rogue's job is to make sure Malande gets as few casts through as possible.

MIND-NUMBING POISON

Maintain Mind-Numbing Poison on Malande at all times.

The reduced casting speed gives the Rogue additional time to react and land interrupts.

KICK

Priority:
1. Divine Wrath
2. Circle of Healing
3. Empowered Smite

DIVINE WRATH
Highest priority. This should not be allowed to cast freely.

CIRCLE OF HEALING
Second priority. Do not allow Malande to repeatedly heal the Council.

EMPOWERED SMITE
Lower priority than Divine Wrath and Circle of Healing, but should still be interrupted whenever possible.

PVP GLOVES

The Rogue should use PvP gloves to provide additional Kick coverage.

The goal is to eliminate missed interrupts and provide as much continuous control as possible.

The Rogue should remain glued to Malande for the entire fight.

If Kick is unavailable:
- Use the available backup interrupt.
- Shaman should be prepared to cover Malande.
- Do not allow Divine Wrath to go through simply because Kick is on cooldown.

BLESSING OF SPELL WARDING

If Gathios places Blessing of Spell Warding on Malande:
- Physical interrupts will not work.
- Shaman and other available magical interrupts must cover her.
- Return to normal Rogue interrupt duty immediately when the shield expires.

REFLECTIVE SHIELD

When Reflective Shield is active:
- Stop damaging Malande.
- Continue monitoring her casts.
- Resume DPS when the shield expires.

The Rogue remains assigned to Malande even while she is protected.


MALANDE POSITIONING

- Malande remains separated from Gathios and Veras.
- Her tank keeps her in a stable position.
- The Rogue remains directly on Malande.
- Do not bring Malande into the Gathios/Veras group.
- Do not attempt to cleave Malande during Week 1.

Objective:
KEEP MALANDE CONTROLLED AND PREVENT HER FROM HEALING.


ZEREVOR

The Mage is responsible for Zerevor.

Zerevor remains completely separated from the main Council group.

The Mage should:
- Spellsteal Dampen Magic.
- Establish threat.
- Kite Zerevor around the room.
- Avoid Arcane Bolt whenever possible.
- Avoid Flamestrike.
- Avoid Blizzard.
- Maintain Dampen Magic.

The Mage's primary responsibility is survival. DPS is secondary.


CHROMATIC RESISTANCE AURA

Gathios can cast Chromatic Resistance Aura.

For Week 1, we are intentionally keeping Malande outside Gathios's aura whenever positioning allows.

Casters should still be prepared for the aura when attacking Gathios and Veras.

Curse of the Elements should remain active.

The 162 Spell Penetration figure comes from:
250 Resistance
- 88 Curse of the Elements
= 162 remaining resistance

This is an optimization rather than a reason to complicate the Week 1 strategy.


GATHIOS

Gathios remains with Veras.

Tank him away from Malande.

CONSECRATION
- Move Gathios out.
- Keep the raid out.
- Maintain Gathios/Veras positioning.

JUDGEMENT
- Prepare for heavy tank damage.
- Use defensive cooldowns when appropriate.

BLESSING OF SPELL WARDING
If Gathios protects another Council member:
- Do not waste magical attacks into the protected target.
- Continue attacking an available target.

If Malande receives Spell Warding:
- Interrupt coverage becomes even more important.
- Use Shaman or other available interrupts as necessary.


VERAS

Veras is the primary physical DPS target.

His low armor makes him extremely vulnerable to physical damage.

DEADLY POISON
- Healers immediately prioritize affected players.
- Players use Healthstones and defensives if necessary.

ENVENOM
- Prepare for the burst following Deadly Poison.
- Do not allow affected players to sit at low health.

VANISH

When Veras disappears:
- Swap to Gathios.
- Continue DPS.
- Prepare for Veras's return.

When Veras returns:
- Off Tank immediately snaps threat.
- Return Veras to the Gathios position.
- Resume DPS on Veras.


HEALING PRIORITIES

1. Malande Tank
2. Gathios Tank
3. Veras Tank
4. Deadly Poison targets
5. Mage Tank
6. General raid damage

WEEK 1 SUCCESS CRITERIA

DO NOT WORRY ABOUT OPTIMAL CLEAVE.

The objective is a clean first kill.

Priorities:
1. Malande does not heal the Council.
2. Zerevor does not kill the Mage.
3. Veras does not kill random raid members.
4. Gathios remains controlled.
5. Veras is burned whenever available.
6. Everyone respects mechanics.

Once we have the encounter on farm, we can transition to the optimized strategy:

GATHIOS + VERAS + MALANDE CLEAVE

with only Zerevor separated.
